FAQ

Is Modelr a wrapper?

No. Generation always runs on your Mac, and your images are never uploaded. It works fully offline.

Do my images get uploaded?

No. Inputs and outputs stay in a local folder on your disk.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Modelr works fully offline, on both Free and Pro. Nothing leaves your Mac.

What do I need?

An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) on macOS 14 or later. More memory allows larger models.

What can I export?

A standard 3D mesh for Blender, Unreal, Unity, or a slicer.

Does it generate textures?

Yes, on Pro. Pro paints a full color texture from your image. Free generates the shape (geometry) only.

What models does it use?

Modelr is built on Hunyuan3D 2 for shape and Hunyuan3D 2.1 Paint for texturing, all running on your Mac.

Will Free nag me to upgrade?

No. No ads or pop-ups, just an Unlock Pro button in Settings. Free stays free forever.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. One-time purchase, free updates.
